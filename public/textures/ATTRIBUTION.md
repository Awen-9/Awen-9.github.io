# Earth textures

The Earth surface, specular, city-light, and cloud textures are sourced
from the Three.js example texture collection. The underlying Earth imagery is
derived from public scientific datasets, including NASA imagery.

Source: https://threejs.org/examples/textures/planets/

The natural-color daytime Earth texture is provided by Solar System Scope and
used under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license.

Source: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
